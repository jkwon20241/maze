MÆZE

A wayfinding prototype for the Providence Athenæum, by ÆEEEE

MÆZE treats the Athenæum not as a repository to be searched but as a labyrinth to be wandered. Six questions lead you from the card catalog, through the stacks and into the pages of one book, narrowing some 176,800 volumes down to a single word.

  • Click buttons, drawers, cards, spines and words
  • Drag along sliders and lists of lines
  • Swipe to turn pages and flip cards
  • Esc to go back

Made for the hand.
Scan to walk it on your phone.
