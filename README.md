# MÆZE

A touch prototype by ÆEEEE about the Providence Athenæum. Six questions take you from the card catalog to one word in one book.

**Open it:** https://jkwon20241.github.io/maze/

Works on phones, tablets and desktop and fills the whole screen like an app. For no browser bars at all, open it in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Everything responds to touch:

- **Tap** buttons, drawers, cards, spines, the ladder, words and chips. Taps that land just outside a small target still count.
- **Drag** along the sliders and down the list of lines to scrub through them.
- **Swipe** the Book of lines to turn pages, and swipe the index card or the book covers to flip them.
- **Back:** use the Back button, your browser's back, or swipe back from the screen edge.
- Tap an empty spot to see every tappable area flash.

The screens and component states are rendered from the team's Figma prototype ("MÆZE App Design"). The interactions, timers and links follow it.
